# Product overview

## Existing, verified functionality

Only `.gitattributes` is tracked at audit commit `5d12b73`. No product implementation exists. See README for audit scope and method. Nothing can be classified as a working or partially implemented feature.

## Proposed functionality

Purpose: make personal health history understandable and support informed conversations with qualified professionals. Build an editorial, empathetic companion rather than a generic medical dashboard. Avoid assumptions that all women menstruate, seek pregnancy, or share health goals.

MVP: account and consent, symptom records, editable history, private journal, optional menstrual records, reviewed resources, and user-reviewed appointment summaries. Add descriptive observations after safe persistence. Exclude diagnosis, treatment advice, emergency monitoring and fertility guarantees.

Measure logging completion, correction success, ability to find/delete records, summary accuracy, comprehension of limitations, and accessibility task completion. Do not optimize for disclosure volume. Use privacy-reviewed aggregate metrics only.

## Decisions still needed

Research cohort, adult/minor eligibility, launch countries/languages, initial health domains, business model, clinical ownership and measurable success thresholds. Developer timezone does not establish launch jurisdiction.
